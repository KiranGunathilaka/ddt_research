Falcon DDT docs

Research and design notes for Falcon's Formula Student UK FS-AI entry in the DDT class, on the IMechE ADS-DV.

Plan

What we are building and how we will test it.

DDT Architecture and TestsSystem architecture and the final hardware: Hesai ATX, Xsens MTi-8 kit with its ZED-F9P, ZED2, Advantech MIO-5379, CAN and power from the ADS-DV, one GNSS clock, and an Orin NX option for the full ZED SDK. Also vehicle profiles, the staged test plan, FS-AI results, what prior teams fitted, why the car needs cone colour, the ADS-DV vehicle model and landed cost in LKR.6 Nov 2026

Findings

The research behind the plan.

Lidar StatisticsGazebo statistics for the ADS-DV lidar choice: 9 lidars drive the same rule-legal lap with four hairpins, every frame of each simulated point cloud is counted on every cone, and how far ahead each one knows the track is shown in full, uncapped, with the screen recordings.8 Nov 2026 DDT Compute OptionsWhich computer to fit to the ADS-DV: the 2026 FSG Driverless Cup field sorted by computer, GPU and camera; what 42 team code bases use a GPU for; the ZED2 without an NVIDIA GPU; and the Advantech MIO-5379 against the other options, with landed cost in LKR.6 Nov 2026 Lidar Coverage and HairpinsMulti-lidar teams, the 2026 FSG Driverless Cup field sorted by lidar type and channel count, how 120° lidar teams handle tight hairpins in their code, the FS-AI and FSUK track rules checked line by line and drawn to scale, and a 2D and 3D model of a Hesai ATX (120° × 20°) and a VLP-16 on the ADS-DV with a track builder held to the rules.6 Nov 2026 Lidar Resolution and SLAMTheory for choosing Falcon's lidar: rays and rings on a cone against range, what landmark SLAM needs from one scan, how far ahead the car must see at speed, why a 120° lidar loses the inside of a tight turn, and whether the car stays localised through the hairpin. Interactive, with every number computed in the page.5 Nov 2026
