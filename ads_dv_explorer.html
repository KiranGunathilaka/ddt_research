Drive, steering, brakes & batteries

What moves the car, and what the AI can command

Drive

  • One Saietta 119R-68 motor drives the rear axle through a 3.5:1 belt and an open differential Spec §2 HiL
  • 55.7 Nm peak motor torque, 4000 rpm max, 5 kW max electrical power Spec §2
  • 55.7 Nm × 3.5 = 195 Nm, which is the axle torque limit in the CAN requests SIS p.4
  • Kinematic ceiling at 4000 rpm: 4000 / 3.5 × π × 0.515 m ≈ 111 km/h. This is arithmetic, not a rated top speed.
  • Sevcon Gen4 DC controller: CANopen, torque or speed control, IP66, 2 kg datasheet
  • The belt drives sit under the bodywork Inspection §4

Steering

  • DC Electronics Microsteer motor with a Shiftec ZE102-1 controller Spec §3
  • Microsteer EPAS: 13.8 V nominal, up to 37 Nm column torque, 80 A max, 860°/s no-load, 4.8 kg, IP67, CAN control datasheet
  • The AI requests a road-wheel angle (bicycle model) of −21.0 to +21.0°; positive turns left (ISO 8855) SIS p.4 HiL
  • The VCU will not enter AS_DRIVING unless the actual angle is under 5° SIS p.14

Brakes & EBS

  • Hydraulic brakes by wire: separate front and rear pressure requests, 0–100 % SIS p.5
  • EBS states: unavailable, armed, triggered. It must be armed before AS_READY; a pump charges it at start-up ("EBS Init → Charging → Armed") UM §6.2
  • Asking for torque and brake pressure at the same time is a brake plausibility fault and triggers an emergency stop SIS §4
  • Before storage, discharge the brakes from the touchscreen Service page. A normally closed valve keeps the lines locked UM §7.2

Batteries & low voltage

  • Traction: lead acid, 4 × 12 V 100 Ah, 50.0 V nominal, 48.0–52.0 V operating, 5.10 kWh, 200 A peak, 100 A continuous Spec §4
  • Do not operate below 47.5 V; charge to at least 50.0 V before storage UM §5.2 UM §8
  • Charging runs from about 52 V to 58 V and from −25 A down to about −2 A; you can stop below 5 A or above 56 V UM §8.2
  • The LV battery is charged from the traction battery by a DC-DC converter and should read 13.5–14.5 V; below 13.0 V, stop and contact IMechE UM §5.2
  • Power the car on for 15 minutes each week to recharge the LV battery Inspection §5
  • Charger: TC Charger HK-J-H66-40; vehicle power lead uses an Amphenol ELP02A04 connector 2021 manual §2.6
  • The battery positions are not published.

Mounting rules for your sensors and computer

FS-AI 2026 rules T1 and the CAD README
  • T1.1.2–T1.1.3: the only modifications allowed are mounting sensors and the AI computer, and they must not affect any electrical or moving parts of the car Rules p.10
  • T1.1.6: on a shared car you may fit your own computer and extra sensors after IMechE approval Rules p.10
  • T1.3.1–T1.3.2: sensors securely mounted; any edge a pedestrian could touch needs at least a 1 mm radius Rules p.11
  • T1.3.3: sensors must sit inside the surface from the top of the TSAL to the outside edges of the four tyres, or within 100 mm of the original bodywork. Toggle "T 1.3.3 sensor envelope" in the 3D view Rules p.11 CAD README
  • T1.3.4: only owners of their own ADS-DV may use the zone up to 500 mm high and less than 700 mm ahead of the front tyres, no wider than the front axle. Shared cars may not. Rules p.11
  • T1.4: sensors must meet local law (e.g. laser eye safety); prove it with datasheets in the ASF Rules p.11
  • Sensor plate: nominal plate 100 mm above the chassis on the spacer posts; M6 rods with RS 188-3461 quick-release knobs (Ø40); use at least 3 of the 4 posts; Ø6.5 holes recommended; plate at most 5 mm thick; the centre post was removed for 2026 CAD README
  • Wiring: the vehicle-side harness can move within the connector zone; strain relief is needed on both sides (a screwed cable-tie base is recommended) CAD README
  • Approval pack: CAD placed in the official model, mounting parts, fasteners, materials, wiring and strain relief, zipped with a team ID and version CAD README

Safety controls & indicators

What stops the car, and what the lights mean

E-stop loop

The loop is closed only when all of these are true UM §5.2 Glossary:

  • RES active (button twisted up, red LED on)
  • Both side emergency stops "Out"
  • MPP dongle present
  • Inertia switch closed (reset by pressing it down under the front body panel) UM §9

Remote Emergency Stop (RES)

  • GrossFunk transmitter and an in-car receiver; NiMH battery lasts about 4 h 2021 manual §2.2
  • Radio band 430–440 MHz; airport RF has interfered before Op. Safety §8
  • The ASR wears it strapped round the waist whenever the car is active Op. Safety §6.2
  • "Go" switch 0 → 1 (rising edge) starts AS_DRIVING; switching Go off while driving triggers the EBS SIS §3.2, §3.7
  • On CAN: RES_TPDO 181h (E-stop, Go, K3, radio strength) DBC

Switches

  • LVMS (low voltage), ASMS (autonomous system), TSMS (tractive system): three key switches on the right-hand side 2021 manual §3.1
  • AI Power Switch: ON/OFF toggle on the left of the rear panel; powers the compute platform; keep it off in storage even with no computer fitted 2026 photo UM §6.2 UM §6.2
  • 4.3" touchscreen on the right-hand side: mission select (AMI), diagnostics, battery and brake pages, Service page 2021 manual §3.3

Operating area

  • Static work: a hazardous area of at least 5 m around the active car Op. Safety §5
  • Dynamic area at least 30 × 50 m, including a 10 m run-off zone; if the car enters the run-off zone the ASR must hit the RES immediately Op. Safety §6.1
  • No tethered connections while driving: wireless only Op. Safety §6.2
  • No running in rain, standing water, ice or snow Op. Safety §7
  • Lift only with the Quick Lift Jack under the chassis, never by the bodywork, nose or suspension; move it on the skateboard trolleys; avoid slopes over 10 % Op. Safety §4 Transport §4

Handshake

The VCU toggles HANDSHAKE in 520h and the AI must copy it back in 510h. If the copy does not match within 100 ms (10 messages), or any of the five AI messages misses 10 in a row, the VCU raises AI_COMMS_LOST. That means an emergency stop in any state other than AS_OFF. SIS §2.4

Rule T1.1.8 says the VCU needs the correct handshake response within 50 ms, stricter than the SIS's 100 ms. Design for 50 ms. Rules p.10

Autonomous state machine

How the car moves from OFF to DRIVING, and back
From → toCondition
AS_OFF → AS_READYTSMS on, ASMS on, mission chosen on the touchscreen (the AI echoes MISSION_STATUS = 1), EBS armed
AS_READY → AS_DRIVINGAt least 5 s in READY; torque requests 0; steer request 0; DIRECTION_REQUEST = neutral; actual steer under 5°; then a RES Go rising edge. Requests are ignored while READY.
AS_DRIVING → AS_FINISHEDThe AI stops the car with the service brake, then sets MISSION_STATUS = 3; the VCU confirms the car is at rest. Commands are ignored afterwards.
AS_FINISHED → AS_OFFASMS off
AS_READY → AS_OFFASMS off
AS_READY → EMERGENCY_BRAKEShutdown circuit opens: RES E-stop, RES link lost, side E-stop, inertia switch, VCU critical fault, BMS fault, or MPP dongle removed
AS_DRIVING → EMERGENCY_BRAKEAny of the above, or Go switched off, ASMS off, or a fault below
AS_FINISHED → EMERGENCY_BRAKEShutdown circuit opens
EMERGENCY_BRAKE → AS_OFFAfter the 15 s braking timer, ASMS off. A power cycle is needed before driving again.

Faults that stop the car in AS_DRIVING

  • MISSION_STATUS_FAULT: MISSION_STATUS = finished while any wheel is above 10 rpm
  • AUTONOMOUS_BRAKING_FAULT: DIRECTION_REQUEST = neutral while any wheel is above 10 rpm
  • AI_COMMS_LOST: handshake or message timeout
  • BRAKE_PLAUSIBILITY_FAULT: torque request above 0 together with a brake request above 0

Source: SIS §3–4. Rule T4.2.2 also requires the AI to request the EBS itself when it detects an error, for example a lost sensor. Rules p.13

Operating the car

Abridged from the 2026 User Manual
  1. Inspect the car; check tyre pressures (20–22 psi), wheel nuts, brake lines and steering lock to lock Inspection §4
  2. AI Power Switch off; fit the MPP dongle; RES on (twist up, red LED); side E-stops out UM §5.2
  3. LVMS on → TSAL green → touchscreen starts → E-stop loop green; LV 13.5–14.5 V; traction above 47.5 V UM §5.2
  4. Touchscreen shows AS Init, OP Init, TS Init, EBS Init UM §5.2
  5. Computer on a power + CAN plug; AI Power Switch on; ASMS and TSMS on UM §6.2
  6. The computer starts sending CAN. As soon as valid messages arrive, the tractive system and EBS activate: TSAL red, EBS Init → Charging → Armed, AS Init → AS Off UM §6.2
  7. Insert the GEMS DA3 logger card (two green LEDs); select a mission and press Set → AS Ready (red + yellow) UM §6.2
  8. After 5 s blue flashes too; the ASR steps away and flips Go 0 → 1 → AS Driving UM §6.2
  9. After every mission (Finished or Emergency Brake), switch LVMS, ASMS and TSMS off to reset UM §6.2

Charging, storage (with brake discharge) and RES battery charging are in UM §7–10.

Missions & scrutineering

What the software must run

AMI_STATE values: 1 Acceleration · 2 Skidpad · 3 Autocross · 4 Track drive · 5 Static inspection A · 6 Static inspection B · 7 Autonomous demo. The AI must read the AMI correctly from the VCU (T4.3.2). SIS p.6 Rules p.13–14

  • Static inspection A (car on stands): sweep the full steering range and finish straight; ramp the drivetrain to 200 rpm in 10 s, stop within 5 s, set AS Finished
  • Static inspection B: spin to 50 rpm, then trigger the EBS so the car goes to AS Emergency
  • Autonomous demo before any dynamic event
  • Inspection items: a paper ASF (8 pages max), datasheets for all perception sensors, proof they meet local law Rules IN2, p.15–16

The DDT scrutineering document is still a stub. Planned content: sticker sheet, sensor CAD review, ASR quiz, HiL CAN comms check (boot and SocketCAN launch script), sensor visual review, static and dynamic inspections. DDT Scrutineering

Discrepancies found while checking

Where the sources disagree
  • Handshake timeout: SIS says 100 ms; rule T1.1.8 says 50 ms. SIS §2.4 Rules p.10
  • Front drive messages on a RWD car: the 2026 spec is single-motor RWD, but the CAN spec (from the 2021 car) still has front and rear drive messages (511h, 512h). The FS-AI API exposes one axle torque request. Spec fs-ai_api.h
  • Protocol change warning: rule T1.1.4 warns that the 2026 protocol "may not be backwards compatible", while the 2026 manual says the interface is unchanged. Rules p.10 UM §6
  • DBC file name: the SIS markdown links ADS-DV_2025_CAN1.dbc; the repository holds ADS-DV_2026_CAN1.dbc. SIS md
  • ASSI blue flashing: the 2021 manual says it means EMERGENCY_BRAKE; the 2026 manual uses it for "5 s timer elapsed" in AS_READY. 2021 §3.2 UM 2026
  • Track: spec 1200 mm; CAD wheel centres 1183 mm. The 2° camber accounts for the gap at the contact patch.
  • Tyres in the simulator: the HiL model uses RT_185_50R13, not the spec 225/45 R13. HiL tyres
  • InCarPC GPU name: listed as "GTX 1060 Ti", a model NVIDIA never sold. 1280 CUDA cores matches the GTX 1060 6 GB. README
  • InCarPC Ethernet ports: the spec lists "2x GbE LAN ports"; IMechE's own photos also show LAN 3–10 marked PoE on the rear face, ten RJ45 in all. README spec photos
  • Rear panel: the 2021 car had a rotary AI master switch knob and a separate "AI computer ignition" toggle beside the MPP and charge port. The 2026 photos show one ON/OFF toggle, the AI Power Switch, with the MPP and charge port, and the InCarPC now carries its own ignition toggle on its plate. 2021 manual Fig. 13 2026 photo InCarPC spec
  • Front USB count: the Compute README describes the 2025 wiring as one front USB 3.0 port, shared with the ZED2; the 2026 CAD README lists two sockets. Compute README CAD README
  • Legacy node name: the 2026 DBC still calls the AI node "NVIDIA Drive PX2". DBC
  • Battery chemistry: the 2021 manual ends charging at a 3.55 V maximum cell voltage (a lithium-type cell); the 2026 spec says lead acid, 4 × 12 V. Use the 2026 numbers. 2021 manual §8 Spec §4
  • Old AI interface: the 2018 IMechE electrical interface sheet (two Deutsch DTP power plugs, 25 A each, and a 9-way D-sub for CAN) and the 2018 drawing (4WD, Avon tyres, lidar in the nose) describe the original car. The 2026 car uses the 7W2 plug and is RWD. FS2023 archive

About the 3D model. Parts are placed three ways: from the official 2026 CAD (exact), from IMechE photos and drawings of earlier versions of the car (approximate, solid), or, for internals whose position IMechE does not publish (battery, VCU, motor, steering motor, brake unit, DC-DC, fuse), as dashed ghost boxes in plausible spots. The Computer buttons switch between the shared InCarPC and your own computer at the rear or on the front plate; purple dashed boxes stand for your hardware and are placeholders, not size limits. InCarPC ports are placed from IMechE's photos of its two faces. Cable routes inside the car are schematic: the documents say what connects to what, not the harness path. The "Simplified" model is drawn by this page from dimensions and a coarse bodywork outline measured from the official CAD. Suspension members are placed from the CAD's part bounding boxes, so treat them as indicative. "Load official CAD" downloads the STEP files from IMechE's GitHub and renders them in your browser. The CAD is © IMechE and its README asks that it is not re-uploaded to other public locations, so this page does not contain it. CAD README Markers with a dashed ring are parts the documents describe but the CAD does not contain. Their positions come from the text or IMechE renders, so they are approximate.
